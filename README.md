Zeno is a great instinct — the paradoxes angle is perfect for a backtracking search engine. Let's riff on that specific vein:

**Other paradox philosophers**
- **Eubulid** – trimmed version of Eubulides, less googleable
- **Buridan** – as in Buridan's Ass, the famous indecision paradox
- **Chrysipp** – trimmed Chrysippus, unusual enough to be free
- **Megarian** – the Megarian school of logic, very obscure

**Zeno's paradoxes themselves**
- **Achilles** – the most famous Zeno paradox
- **Dichotomy** – another Zeno paradox name
- **Fletched** – from the "arrow paradox" (a fletched arrow)
- **Stadion** – Zeno's least known paradox, almost certainly unclaimed

**Same vibe, different era**
- **Aporix** – from "aporia," the Greek term for a logical puzzle or impasse
- **Aporex** – same root, more product-y feel
- **Elenchus** – Socratic method of refutation through logic

**My honest best bet:** **Stadion** or **Aporix** — obscure enough to be unclaimed, but with a solid intellectual pedigree you could tell a good story about.
